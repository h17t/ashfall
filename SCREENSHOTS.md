# Mournwake — screenshots

Captured headless at 1400×900 from the built game (`npm run build && npx vite preview`), by the scripts in `scripts/`. Every frame is the live UI: no compositing, no retouching. The three the brief names first.

## The three that have to sit on a Steam page

| | |
|---|---|
| ![Boss intro](art/final/boss-intro.png) | **Boss intro.** the Pyre-Warden, Warden of the Cold Pyre, arriving: letterbox, the name card with its mote rule, the epithet, the lore; the page beyond the frame steps back. Unskippable the first time you meet him. |
| ![Reprisal](art/final/reprisal.png) | **The reprisal moment.** The window is open: the figure rim-lit in mote, the chromatic edge, the display-face numbers, the composure gauge blown. |
| ![Lantern](art/final/lantern.png) | **The road, at rest.** The Cindered Approach under an ash sky, the hub slab overlapping the frame, a hint on torn parchment. |
