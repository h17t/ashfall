# Mournwake — the fiction

Everything in the game is written against this page. If an item description, a boss epithet or a
line of interface copy contradicts it, the copy is wrong.

## The premise

You are a **Revenant**: dead, and refusing it.

You woke at your own grave with a **Lantern** in your hand. Its flame is the only thing holding you
in the world; when it gutters you feel yourself thin. Nobody lit it for you. Nobody remembers who
you were, and the people who buried you did not stay.

The land is a funeral that never finished. The mourners went home; the dead did not. What is left
walks the road in procession, and everything that walks it wants what you carry.

## Marrow

Every living thing keeps a little of the world's heat in its bones. The dead keep it longer. When
you break something on the road, its **Marrow** comes loose, and a Revenant can take it in.

Marrow is the currency and the reason. You spend it to grow: to make your body remember Vitality,
Breath, Might, Finesse, Insight, Devotion. You spend it to make the smith remember his work.

When you die you wake again at your grave, and the Marrow you carried lies where you fell in a pale
heap: **your Remains**. Go back for them. Die on the way and the road eats them.

## The Lantern

The Lantern is the checkpoint, the hub and the light. Resting at it refills your **Tallowdraught**,
the rendered-fat cordial that closes wounds, and calls your Cortege to heel.

Each region of the road has a place where a Lantern will take; lighting it is how you claim a
stretch of road as somewhere you can wake.

## The Cortege

Behind you walks your **Cortege**: a funeral procession of **Shades**, the dead who found your
light and chose to follow it rather than the road. They fight beside you. When you rest, they hunt
on. When you are gone from the world (the app is closed), they hunt still, and bring back what
they can carry.

A Shade that is lost is not nothing. What it was becomes an **Echo** in the Lantern's light: a
small permanent gift from someone who walked with you a while.

## Creeds

The dead organise. Five **Creeds** hold to five different answers to the question of what a
Revenant is for, and each will lend its answer to one who swears it. You may hold one Creed at a
time. Standing with a Creed is never lost, even when the Lantern is snuffed.

## The three schools

- **Weaving**: the pulling of Marrow into shape. Insight's art. Needs a Weaver's Staff.
- **Litany**: words said over the dead until the world agrees. Devotion's art. Needs Litany Beads.
- **Ruin**: heat let out of the bones all at once. Scales with the **Ruin Brand** you feed Marrow
  to, and a little with Insight and Devotion.

Spells are held in **Recitation slots**: what you can keep in mind at once.

## Composure, Break, Reprisal

Everything that fights has **Composure**. Wear it down and it **Breaks**: a moment of openness in
which a **Reprisal** lands for many times its worth. The whole rhythm of the road is this: wait,
wear, break, answer.

## Keepsakes

When a lord of the road falls it leaves a **Keepsake**: the last dense knot of what it was. A
Keepsake can be worked into a weapon or spoken into a spell. Either. Not both. The other is lost.

## Snuffing the Lantern

When the road has nothing left to teach you, you may **Snuff the Lantern**. The flame goes out; the
world goes dark; and you wake again at your grave in a darker one, carrying a **Vestige** of what
you learned. The Vestige buys permanent things: the body remembering faster, the flask running
deeper, the Cortege hunting further.

The first waking is the one you started in. Snuff the Lantern and you enter **the Second Waking**,
then the Third. Each is harder and pays more.

## The Severing and the Unmaking

Deep in the wakings there is a second, worse door. **The Severing** cuts the **Threads** that tie
you to the road you have walked; you keep what you know, and lose what you had, and gain a way to
work the world itself. Beyond it lies **the Unmaking**: the choice to let the world come apart a
little more each time so that what is left is worth more. The game's last word for death is the
same word: **UNMADE.**

## Tone rules

- The dead are tired, not evil. Bosses are people who kept a promise too long.
- Nothing is hopeful except the player. The light is yours and only yours.
- Lore is transcribed, not narrated: short, specific, in a voice that has seen the thing it
  describes. No "ancient", no "legendary", no capital-letter Evil.
- Every name is a real word or a compound of real words. No invented fantasy spellings.
- Bosses are named article-noun with an epithet: *The Fenwright, Who Drowned the Choir*.
