# Mournwake — QA checklist

What automation cannot judge. Walk this on a real phone (a mid-range Android over 4G, then an
iPhone) before a release, in portrait, one-handed, without pinching or turning the phone. Tick
each line or write what was wrong.

## Install and first minute

- [ ] Opens from the browser in under three seconds on 4G; the arena is the first thing on screen.
- [ ] The first hint says to tap Strike, not to click or press a key.
- [ ] Tapping Strike answers on the next frame: no 300 ms wait, no double-tap zoom, no highlight flash.
- [ ] A finger held on Dodge does not stop a second finger on Strike from landing.
- [ ] The haptic on a hit is a tick you feel and forget; the crit is sharper; nothing buzzes while idle.
- [ ] After the first lord, the install sheet appears once, six seconds later, and never again after Not now.
- [ ] Installed, it opens standalone with the splash, in portrait, with no browser bar.
- [ ] In airplane mode, the installed game opens and plays; the first region's art is there.

## Dying and coming back

- [ ] Die. UNMADE. reads at arm's length; the haptic is long and heavy; the marrow lost is stated.
- [ ] The Remains hint says how to get them back; fighting back through the tiers recovers them.
- [ ] Die again before reaching them: the log says they are gone, and nothing else is.
- [ ] Die on the Stair: the death card says what the stair kept; the purse is untouched; no Remains.

## The hand

- [ ] Every frequent action (Strike, Dodge, Draught, a boon, Withdraw, Take) is in the bottom third.
- [ ] Nothing destructive (Delete everything, Forsake a creed, Remove an order) is in the thumb zone without a confirmation.
- [ ] Every bottom sheet closes on the scrim, its ×, a pull down and Escape, except the boon offer, which has no close and says why.
- [ ] Swiping sideways on a section changes pillar; scrolling a section vertically never changes pillar.
- [ ] Long-pressing any row with details opens its sheet; the same tap that opens it does not also fire the row's action.

## The Stair

- [ ] Floor 1 is a warm-up; by floor 5 the lord is a wall you feel.
- [ ] The offer sheet's cards read in one glance: name, rule, lore; the chosen one fills; Take names the boon.
- [ ] Withdraw always shows the exact sum it banks; the climb-out sheet matches it.
- [ ] A run where three boons line up (say Grave-Momentum, First Cut, Glass Marrow) feels absurd, in the good way.

## Orders, the forge, the Study

- [ ] Writing "WHEN HP below 35% THEN Drink" takes four taps and no typing; it fires within a second of crossing the line.
- [ ] Reordering with the arrows changes which order fires first when both hold.
- [ ] A reforge flashes the rows; a locked affix never changes; the price climbs with each lock.
- [ ] Reaching a Study rank on a creature you fight often lands as a log line and a pip, not a modal.

## The Toll, afflictions, the stretch mechanics

- [ ] The Black Hour is exciting: the header turns blue, the stage darkens, spawns are dark-touched, the pay shows in the log.
- [ ] Coming back after hours, the away report says how much of the absence fell in the Black Hour and that it paid.
- [ ] Turning on Thin Blood is felt within a fight; turning it off lifts it at once.
- [ ] A shade sent into the dark that does not come back leaves an Echo the Modifiers list names.
- [ ] A raid strip appears in Combat with a Go button; five kills there repel it; letting it ride costs at most an hour of slower yield.
- [ ] The war board's bars move between sessions; your side's bonus reads correctly when you are last.
- [ ] The Art button lights when ready, its cooldown counts, and Reprisal Stance visibly doubles the next Reprisal.

## Reading and settings

- [ ] Plain type switches every face to a humanist sans with wider spacing; nothing overflows.
- [ ] Colour-safe statuses: bleed, poison and frost are told apart by shape and by text, not only colour.
- [ ] Reduce effects stops the shake, the flashes and the motes; the fire still moves unless the system asks for reduced motion.
- [ ] The quality control names the detected tier; Battery drops the grain and the ambient motes.
- [ ] Turning the phone sideways shows the fight beside the section; turning back loses nothing (tab, scroll, sheet).

## Interruption

- [ ] Background the app mid-fight, kill it from the task switcher, reopen: the save is at most a moment old and every kill is there.
- [ ] Leave it for two hours: the away report opens with the right hours; the Toll clock has moved; expeditions came home.
